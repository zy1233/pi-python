# Find your past chats

The **Past chats** list is under the chat. It shows the chats that were held in this folder, newest first, each with the first message and the time of its last message.

- Select a chat to open it. Its history shows in the chat, and you can go on.
- Select the trash button of a chat to delete it. A question asks first.
- A chat appears in the list after its first message.
- The list shows the chats of the open folder only.
